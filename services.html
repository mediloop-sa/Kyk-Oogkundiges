What we do

Services built around your eyes

From a first pair of glasses to specialised learning-related vision support — here's how we look after you and your family.

General eye care

Comprehensive sight tests and eye health checks using advanced, comfortable technology — for adults and children alike.

  • Routine & medical aid eye tests
  • Digital retinal imaging
  • Ongoing eye health monitoring
  • Prescription updates for glasses and contact lenses

Eyewear & contact lenses

A curated frame selection and expert fitting, with flexible in-store payment options.

  • Frame styling and fitting for every face and budget
  • Children's contact lens fitting
  • 3-month interest-free payment via Payflex

Children's vision & dyslexia evaluations

We offer comprehensive children's vision assessments tailored to individual needs, from six months old.

  • Paediatric vision evaluations from 6 months
  • Dyslexia evaluations (English & Afrikaans) with a detailed SGDA report approved by the Department of Education
  • Computerised infra-red eye tracking to assess pursuit and saccade movements, reading speed, binocular function and gaze
  • Regressions and fixations for detailed reading analysis
  • Read EZ / Meares-Irlen colorimetry and reading evaluation for visual stress
  • School vision screening

Vision therapy

Customised therapy programmes, including a home program, for visually related learning difficulties.

  • Test of Visual Perceptual Skills
  • Left-right reversals evaluation
  • Developmental Eye Movement Test
  • Individualised, at-home follow-up exercises
